Record operating costs, then use reports to understand gym sales, visits, and finances. Start with the same period and branch to compare figures accurately.

## Record an expense

1. Create categories in **Pengeluaran → Kategori Pengeluaran**, such as rent or electricity.
2. Open **Pengeluaran → Daftar Pengeluaran** and add an expense already paid.
3. Enter the branch, category, date, payee, description, amount, and payment method.
4. Add a reference for non-cash payments.
5. Review and save.

This record does not initiate a bank transfer. Use a payment method enabled for suppliers or both purposes at the selected branch.

Recorded expenses cannot be edited directly. To correct a mistake, cancel with a reason and enter the correct expense. Cancellation also affects reports for the original period.

## Expenses paid from the cash drawer

<Note>
  An expense records a business cost, while **Kas Keluar** records cash taken from the drawer. If paying from the drawer, record both so expense reports and shift balances remain accurate.
</Note>

## Choose a report

| Laporan Gym report | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Penjualan | Review sales transactions and refunds. |
| Membership | Review member membership periods. |
| Kunjungan | Review arrival records. |
| Pengeluaran | Check recorded operating costs. |
| Laba Rugi | Review business results from available data. |
| Arus Kas | Trace money received and paid out. |
| Rekonsiliasi Kas | Review shift closing results and cash differences. |

## Read and export reports

1. Open the relevant report.
2. Select the date range and branch.
3. Apply search terms if needed.
4. Read the summary alongside the table details.
5. Export CSV when available and permitted.

Summaries cover the filtered results, not just rows on the visible table page. Exports follow the filters applied.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why might report figures differ?">
  Check these in order:

  1. **Branch and dates:** make sure the filters match.
  2. **Type of figure:** sales, money received, and cash balances mean different things.
  3. **Transaction corrections:** check refunds and cancelled expenses.
  4. **Recording time:** transactions outside the selected period are excluded.

  Open source documents to trace a difference instead of comparing only summary totals.
  </Accordion>
