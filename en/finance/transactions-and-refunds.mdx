Find sales records, reopen receipts, or start a refund through **Penjualan → Transaksi**. Front desk accounts may see this as **Riwayat Transaksi**.

## Find and open a transaction

Find the transaction, open its details, and check the items, customer, payment method, total, and refund status. You can reopen or reprint receipts for recorded transactions.

| Document | Records |
| --- | --- |
| Customer invoice | The customer's amount owed to the gym. |
| Supplier invoice | The gym's amount owed for goods purchased. |
| Refund | Money returned from a fully paid customer transaction. |

## Before a refund

Check the reason and original transaction. Refunds are available only to authorized accounts and eligible items. Read the form's explanation if an item cannot be selected.

## Record a refund

1. Open a paid transaction and choose **Refund**.
2. Select the items and quantities being refunded.
3. For products, return stock only if the goods have been received and are suitable for resale.
4. Select a method, enter the reason, and provide a reference for non-cash refunds.
5. Review the amount calculated by the system.
6. Confirm only after the money has actually been returned or transferred.

<Warning>
  Confirm a refund after returning the funds. Kavosto records refunds; it does not perform bank transfers.
</Warning>

If goods are returned to stock, they go to the original sales warehouse.

## Conditions to check

- Products can be refunded partially or for the entire remaining eligible quantity.
- Memberships must meet usage conditions; a membership already used for check-in cannot be refunded through this flow.
- PT or class packages with used or reserved sessions may be ineligible for refunds.
- When shifts are enabled, cash refunds require the staff member's shift and enough cash in the drawer.

## Review the refund result

The original invoice remains paid to preserve the initial payment record. Partial or full refund status is shown separately. Review the history in transaction details or **Refund**.

If your connection drops, check the history before submitting another request to avoid returning money twice.
