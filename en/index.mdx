Welcome to the Kavosto guide. Learn how to set up your gym, serve members, and keep track of your business in one place.

## New to Kavosto?

Start with the [gym setup guide](/en/getting-started). It walks you through completing your profile, creating membership plans, setting up payments, and giving your team access.

If your gym is already set up, choose a guide for the task you want to complete. You do not need to read everything from the beginning.

## Understand your account access

Menu names in these guides match the application. The English guides keep Indonesian menu and button labels where you need to find them in the app. The menus and data you can see depend on your gym's subscription, your role, and your branch access.

Before you start, check that the correct branch is selected. If a menu is missing, ask your gym owner or administrator to review your permissions.

Screenshots currently show the Indonesian interface; the surrounding instructions explain each step in English.
