Manage gym locations through **Cabang**, then use **Staff** to decide who can work at each location. Access permissions help each team member see the menus and data appropriate to their role.

## Check your branches

Verify the branch identity and status before using it for members, warehouses, schedules, or transactions. Available branch capacity follows the gym subscription.

The active branch selector in the navigation keeps your work focused on the right location. Check it whenever expected data is missing.

## Add a staff account

1. Open **Staff** with an account allowed to manage staff.
2. Enter the staff member's identity and login details.
3. Assign their role and branch access.
4. Review the details before saving.
5. Ask them to sign in using their own account.

Grant access based on responsibilities. Front desk staff need service workflows, while finance staff need the relevant records and reports.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do accounts show different menus?">
  Navigation follows roles and permissions. Front desk accounts have a more focused operational menu. Trainer accounts have their own workspace and **Jadwal Saya**.

  <Note>
    Permission to view data is different from permission to take action. Staff who can open a transaction may not be allowed to confirm, cancel, or refund it.
  </Note>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Update staff access

Review roles and branches when a staff member changes location or responsibilities. When someone leaves, handle any open work, such as an active shift, then deactivate the account according to your gym's policy.

## Trace staff activity

The owner can use **Log Aktivitas** to review recorded changes. When investigating an issue, note the document number, branch, time, and staff account to make it easier to find the activity.
