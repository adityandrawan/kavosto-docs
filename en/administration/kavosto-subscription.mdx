Manage application access, features, and gym capacity through **Langganan**. Use the owner's account to select a plan, renew, or change capacity.

<Note>
  A Kavosto subscription is for the gym. For your customers' gym access plans, open [Memberships](/en/operations/memberships).
</Note>

## Activate your subscription

1. Sign in with the gym owner's account.
2. Open **Langganan**, or follow the activation button on the access restriction page.
3. Check the plan, number of branches, and billing period.
4. Review the price summary, then continue to checkout.
5. Follow the available payment instructions.
6. Check the subscription status again after payment has been processed.

**Menunggu aktivasi** means operational access is not active yet. Check the payment status and follow the next steps shown on the subscription page.

## Choose a plan and capacity

Compare features and usage limits on the plan cards. Use **Lihat semua fitur** to expand the feature list.

Check the number of branches and total allowances before continuing. Choose capacity that fits your gym's needs.

## Change your plan or branch count

Choose **Ubah paket & cabang**, select the new plan or capacity, and read **Ringkasan perubahan**.

| Effective time | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Immediately | The change cost and payment steps. |
| Next period | The start date and payment information. |

Follow the effective time shown in the summary for your selection.

Selecting the same terms as your current subscription does not create a change. To continue with the same plan, use the available renewal flow.

## Review the cost of a change

A mid-period change may account for the new plan's remaining period and credit from the remaining subscription. Use the checkout total as your payment reference because it may differ from the plan's monthly price.

If the payment deadline has passed, reopen the latest summary before paying.
