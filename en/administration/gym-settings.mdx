Configure how your gym works through **Pengaturan**. Use the owner's account and check the selected branch before changing branch-specific settings.

## Choose the right settings

| Menu | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Profil Gym | Gym identity and general settings, including time zone. |
| Metode Pembayaran | Methods for customer payments and supplier payment records. |
| Pajak | Tax rules used for sales. |
| Kasir | Shift mode and cashier terminals. |
| Struk | Transaction receipt appearance and size. |
| Check-in | Check-in only or check-in with check-out. |
| Persediaan | Initial gym-wide FIFO or Average setting; locked after the first stock movement. |
| Pesan Member | Automated member message templates and delivery channels. |
| Pustaka Media | Settings related to gym media usage. |

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A payment method is missing">
  Check that the method is active, applies to the selected branch, and is enabled for customers, suppliers, or both. A supplier method is not automatically available for customer payments at the point of sale.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Before changing operational modes

- Close all branch shifts before changing cashier mode.
- Close all open visits before disabling check-out.
- Save the [inventory valuation method](/en/products-inventory/inventory#set-up-warehouses-and-valuation) before the first stock movement; once locked, it cannot be changed through settings.

## Time zone

Use the time zone appropriate for your gym. Read visit times, schedules, and report date boundaries in the context of the gym's time zone.

## Save and verify

After changing settings, save and wait for confirmation. If another user has already changed the data, reload, review the latest values, and decide which changes are still needed.

Continue to [Cashier shifts](/en/operations/cashier-shifts), [Check-in](/en/operations/check-in), or [Member messages](/en/administration/member-messages).
