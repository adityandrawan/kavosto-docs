description: "Record supplier invoices, due dates, and partial or full payments."

Record supplier invoices, monitor due dates, and log payments through **Pembelian → Tagihan Supplier**. Start with a confirmed purchase order.

## Create a supplier invoice

1. Choose **Buat tagihan supplier** from a purchase, or add an invoice and select its source purchase.
2. Enter the supplier invoice number, date, and due date.
3. Match item quantities and prices to the supplier's invoice.
4. Check discounts, shipping, extra charges, and any tax amount.
5. Save a draft for review, or choose **Simpan & konfirmasi** when correct.

One purchase can have multiple invoices. Confirmed quantities cannot exceed the remaining billable order quantities. The supplier invoice number must be unique for that supplier.

<Note>
  Supplier invoice tax is entered as a rupiah amount. Match the subtotal, deductions, extra charges, and total against the supplier's invoice before confirming.
</Note>

## Record a supplier payment

1. Open the details of a confirmed invoice.
2. Choose **Catat pembayaran**.
3. Enter the date, method, amount, reference, and any required notes.
4. Check the outstanding balance, then confirm after payment has actually been made.
5. Make sure the record appears in **Riwayat Pembayaran**.

Payments can be partial or full. Kavosto records payments already made; use your payment provider to transfer funds to the supplier.

### Example of partial payment

For an invoice of **Rp1,000,000**, you pay **Rp400,000** first. Record it against the same invoice. The remaining balance is **Rp600,000**; record the next payment when it is settled.

## Pay before the goods arrive

An invoice can be paid before delivery as long as the purchase is confirmed. Stock still increases only when a receipt is recorded.

Do not record a goods receipt merely to indicate that payment has been made.

## Read document and payment statuses

**Document status** shows whether an invoice is a draft, confirmed, or cancelled. **Payment status** shows settlement progress. A confirmed invoice is not necessarily paid.

Match **Riwayat Pembayaran** amounts against payment evidence and the remaining balance.

If you opened the list from a single purchase, use **Lihat semua tagihan** to see other invoices again.
