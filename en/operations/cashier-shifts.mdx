description: "Open shifts, record cash movements, and close shifts using physical cash counts."

Use shifts to track cash received, removed, and handed over between staff. The owner configures this mode per branch through **Pengaturan → Kasir**.

## Choose a cashier mode

| Mode | Preparation |
| --- | --- |
| Without shifts | Staff can accept payments without opening a shift. |
| With shifts | Set up terminals and open a shift before accepting payments. |

A terminal is a cashier desk or checkout point, not a staff account. You can change cashier mode only after all shifts at the branch are closed.

## Open a shift

1. Open **Shift Kasir** or follow the prompt from the point of sale.
2. Select the correct branch and terminal.
3. Enter the opening balance based on the physical cash available; zero is allowed.
4. Save and check the active shift summary.

<Note>
  Each terminal and each staff member can have only one active shift. Use individual accounts so transactions are attributed correctly.
</Note>

## Record cash in and cash out

Record cash movements outside customer payments using the shift's cash-in or cash-out action. Enter the amount and explain its purpose.

Cash out for an operating cost is separate from an **Pengeluaran** expense record. When taking money from the drawer to pay a cost, record both as appropriate: cash out for the drawer balance, and an expense for the business cost.

## Close a shift

1. Complete any transaction in progress.
2. Count the physical cash.
3. Open the shift details and choose the close-shift action.
4. Enter the count and review any difference.
5. Complete the requested notes and confirm.

Enter the actual amount counted. If it differs from the expected balance, review transactions and cash history, then explain the difference.

Review closing results in the [cash reconciliation report](/en/finance/reports-and-expenses).
