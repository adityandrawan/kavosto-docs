Manage schedules, participants, and training outcomes through **Jadwal & Layanan**. Use Kelas Gym for group sessions and Personal Trainer for individual training.

| What you need | Manage through |
| --- | --- |
| Session package prices and benefits | **Paket & Layanan → Add-on** |
| Class schedules and participants | **Jadwal & Layanan → Kelas Gym** |
| Trainers and personal training schedules | **Jadwal & Layanan → Personal Trainer** |

## Set up gym classes

1. Open **Jadwal & Layanan → Kelas Gym**.
2. Add a **Jenis kelas**, such as beginner yoga.
3. Create a schedule with the branch, instructor, time, capacity, and participant access rules.
4. For paid packages, prepare a class-session add-on.
5. Open the schedule details and choose **Tambah peserta**.
6. Select the member and package if required, then save the booking.

<Note>
  Buying a session package does not book the member into a schedule. Add a booking so capacity, package validity, and access conditions can be checked.
</Note>

## Set up personal training

1. Make sure the trainer has an active staff account with access to the service branch.
2. Open **Jadwal & Layanan → Personal Trainer**.
3. Complete the trainer profile, available hours, and unavailable dates.
4. Prepare a PT session-package add-on and sell it through the point of sale.
5. Schedule the member's training using the appropriate package.

A paid PT package provides a session balance. Gym membership and PT packages remain separate; check whether the training package requires an active membership.

## Record attendance and training outcomes

Check the schedule, participants, and time before marking attendance, absence, completion, or cancellation. Session usage follows the package rules and selected action.

For classes, close the session after its scheduled time has ended and all participants have an attendance result. Gym check-in does not replace class or PT attendance.

## Transfer remaining balances from older packages

Use **Catat saldo awal** for remaining sessions purchased before they were recorded in Kavosto. Check proof of purchase, activation date, remaining sessions, and expiry before saving.

An opening balance only transfers remaining service rights already purchased. For new sales and payment records, use the [point of sale](/en/operations/point-of-sale).
