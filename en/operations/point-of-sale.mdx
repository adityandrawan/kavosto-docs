Sell products, memberships, and add-ons in one cart through **Kasir**. Once payment succeeds, you can open or print the transaction receipt.

## Before a transaction

- Check the transaction branch and select a sales warehouse for stock-managed goods.
- If the branch uses shifts, open a shift under the account of the person working.
- Make sure the required payment methods are available.

## 1. Prepare the cart

1. Search for items by name, SKU, or barcode.
2. Select an item from the catalog and review its quantity in the cart.
3. For memberships or session packages, complete the member and required terms.
4. Check the items, quantities, and prices before choosing **Bayar**.

Regular products can be sold without selecting a member. Select a member when the purchase needs to be recorded in their name, especially for memberships and session packages.

<Warning>
  An unpaid cart is not retained after reloading the page and does not reserve stock. Complete or empty the cart before changing branches.
</Warning>

## 2. Accept payment

1. Check the total again in the payment dialog.
2. If using a voucher, enter its code and choose **Terapkan**.
3. Select a payment method.
4. For cash, enter the amount received and check the change.
5. For non-cash payments, verify that the funds have arrived before confirming.
6. Confirm payment and wait for the result.

Use one method to pay the entire transaction. Kavosto records non-cash payments; it does not withdraw funds from the customer's bank account.

## Pay an existing invoice

With an empty cart, use **Bayar Tagihan** to find an unpaid invoice. You can also open the invoice details and choose **Terima pembayaran**.

Use the existing invoice to avoid creating a second purchase for the same obligation.

## Print or reopen a receipt

After payment succeeds, choose **Cetak struk**. To reprint, open **Penjualan → Transaksi** and select the transaction.

Receipt size follows **Pengaturan → Struk**: 58 mm or 80 mm. Match the printer paper size, use 100% scale, and disable browser headers and footers if necessary.

## If the payment result is unclear

Check **Penjualan → Transaksi** first. If the transaction exists, open it. If the result is still unclear, retry within the same process and check again before creating a new transaction.

For money returned from a paid transaction, use [Refunds](/en/finance/transactions-and-refunds).
