Prepare a catalog of goods such as bottled water and training equipment through **Produk**. The catalog is used for sales, purchases, and stock records.

For memberships or services, use **Paket & Layanan**.

## Set up the product catalog

1. Create the required units in **Produk → Satuan**.
2. Set up categories in **Produk → Kategori Produk**.
3. Open **Produk → Daftar Produk** and add a product.
4. Enter its identity, SKU, unit, selling price, and other required details.
5. Enable **Kelola stok** if quantities need to be tracked by warehouse.
6. Save and make sure the product appears in the list.

<Note>
  The selling price is used for customer sales. Cost is entered when recording incoming stock. Adding a product to the catalog does not add stock.
</Note>

## Base units and purchase packaging

The base unit is the unit used for stock records. If goods are bought in different packaging, configure a conversion for the product.

### Example of buying cartons

Bottled water uses pcs as its base unit, and one carton contains 24 pcs. Receiving two cartons adds **48 pcs** to stock. Enter the purchase cost per carton when the selected purchase unit is a carton.

Check conversion factors before using them on documents. Units or conversions already used may be locked to preserve transaction history.

## Deactivate a product

Set a product to inactive when it is no longer sold. Products used on documents may not be deletable because their history is still needed for transactions and inventory.

## A product is missing from purchase selection

Make sure it is active and has **Kelola stok** enabled. In the product picker, search by name or SKU and check whether it has already been added to the purchase.

Continue to [Purchase orders](/en/purchasing/purchase-orders) or [Inventory](/en/products-inventory/inventory).
