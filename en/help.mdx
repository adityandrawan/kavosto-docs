Find your issue below and follow the suggested checks. Most missing-data investigations start with the account, active branch, and page filters.

| Issue | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Missing menu | Account role, subscription plan, and branch access. |
| Missing data | Branch, search, status, and dates. |
| Unclear payment result | Transaction details and history. |
| Stock has not increased | Goods receipt and destination warehouse. |
| WhatsApp not received | Delivery history, connection, and member consent. |

## A menu is missing

Menus follow the plan, role, and branch access. Ask the owner to check your permissions. Front desk and trainer accounts may have different navigation from the owner's account.

## Data is missing

Check search terms, status filters, date ranges, and the active branch. Select all statuses when reviewing inactive history. Make sure the list is not limited to one source document.

## Payment was made but the result is unclear

Check transaction details and history before creating another payment. For a Kavosto subscription, check **Langganan**. For customer payments, use **Penjualan → Transaksi** or **Tagihan**.

<Warning>
  Check existing transactions before recording another payment. Keep the document number and payment evidence to help trace the issue.
</Warning>

## A member cannot check in

Check member status, membership payment, validity, and branch access. If check-out is enabled, close the previous visit through the proper exit flow. See [Check-in](/en/operations/check-in).

## Stock has not increased after a purchase

Orders and supplier payments do not add stock. Check that a receipt has been recorded and that you are viewing the destination warehouse. Transferred goods still in transit are not yet part of destination stock.

## A document cannot be edited

Confirmed or posted documents may be locked to preserve history. Use the correction action appropriate to the document. If a notice says another user changed it, reload and check the latest data.

## A WhatsApp message has not arrived

Check the connection, active template, member consent and number, and delivery history. Queued does not mean sent; delivery may follow platform delays and limits. See [Member messages](/en/administration/member-messages).

## Camera or printer problems

For cameras, check browser permissions, HTTPS, and whether another application is using the device. Use manual member search to continue service.

For printers, check receipt size, paper size, print scale, and browser header/footer settings. Try reopening the receipt from the recorded transaction.

## Still need help?

Share the following with your gym manager or support team:

- **Page and action:** what you were doing.
- **Time and branch:** when and where the issue occurred.
- **Document number:** the related transaction, invoice, or order, if available.
- **Error message:** copy the message shown and explain what you expected.

Screenshots can help explain the problem. Hide unnecessary personal information and never include passwords or verification codes.
